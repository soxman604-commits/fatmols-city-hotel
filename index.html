Welcome to Fatmols City

A trusted Ndola address for business and leisure

Fatmols City Hotel sits on the Ndola–Kitwe dual carriageway in the secure Hillcrest area, directly opposite the Levy Mwanawasa Stadium. It is the largest of the Fatmols properties on the Copperbelt and the head office of Fatmols Hotels, a Zambian-owned hospitality company with branches in Ndola, Kansenshi and Lusaka.

Choose from 52 rooms and suites, from Charlotte rooms to our Presidential Suite. Every room comes with satellite TV, a workstation, a telephone and a fridge, so you can work, rest and feel at home.

  • About 10 min drive to Simon Mwansa Kapwepwe International Airport
  • About 5 min drive to the central business district
  • About 4 km to the Copperbelt Museum and Ndola Golf Course
  • About 12 km to the Dag Hammarskjöld Memorial site
